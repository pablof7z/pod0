Restore `cargo build/test/clippy --workspace --all-targets` to green against the actually-committed `pod0-facade`/`pod0-storage` API (git HEAD), closing the HOST-01/HOST-02 gap `01-VERIFICATION.md` found: `pod0-cli`'s committed source (`0e52f794`) calls five `Pod0Facade` methods and imports five re-exported types that exist only in someone else's uncommitted, unrelated `pod0-facade`/`pod0-storage` working-tree edits, never committed to git history. A CI checkout of clean HEAD fails `cargo build --workspace` with 8 compile errors, all in `pod0-cli`.

Per the user's explicit decision, this plan reworks `pod0-cli` to drop the dependency on the not-yet-committed facade APIs. It does not touch `rust/crates/pod0-facade/src/{facade_exports.rs,runtime.rs,runtime_transcript_effect_leases.rs}` or `rust/crates/pod0-storage/src/*` beyond what is already committed at HEAD — those files remain exactly as committed, untouched.

**Important finding from direct investigation of committed HEAD (not assumed from the gap brief):** four of the five missing re-exported types (`LibraryNetworkStep`, `TranscriptProvider`, `ExternalEffectKind`, `DurableExternalEffectRequest`) genuinely exist in committed `pod0_application` — they are just not re-exported through `pod0_facade::facade_exports`. `TranscriptStartPolicy` exists in committed `pod0_domain` (also not re-exported through the facade). These four/five are fixed by importing from their real crate instead of via `pod0_facade`. But `Pod0Facade::create` and `facade.pending_host_effects(...)` are NOT simple renames of committed methods — direct inspection shows the underlying capability (bootstrapping a brand-new authoritative SQLite store from nothing) does not exist anywhere in committed `pod0-storage`/`pod0-facade` at all; it lives entirely in the still-uncommitted `pod0-storage/src/authoritative_bootstrap.rs` (currently an untracked file) and its corresponding uncommitted `facade_exports.rs`/`runtime.rs` edits. `Pod0Facade::open`'s committed implementation opens with `SQLITE_OPEN_READ_ONLY` after a schema-identity check, so it cannot bootstrap a fresh store either. **This means `pod0-cli`'s `create_store` command, and every one of its own committed tests that used `Pod0Facade::create` purely as fixture setup (`tests/host_drain.rs`, `tests/host_pump.rs`, `tests/settings.rs`), cannot be restored to full functional parity within this plan's boundary** (touching `pod0-storage`/`pod0-facade` to add a bootstrap primitive is explicitly out of scope). This plan makes `create_store` fail with an explicit, typed error instead of silently pretending to work, and marks the three now-unrunnable tests `#[ignore]` with a clear, dated reason — a disclosed functional regression, not a silent one. See the `<deliverables>` section below.

Purpose: without this, no CI job checking out a clean commit can ever pass `cargo build --workspace`, which blocks all downstream Phase 2/3/4 work from having a working CI gate.
Output: `pod0-cli` compiles (`--all-targets`, i.e. including tests) against committed HEAD alone, with every previously-uncommitted-facade-dependent call site replaced by either a real committed equivalent or an explicit, disclosed scope reduction.
